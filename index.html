How to Play

Cross your own trail to close a loop. Everything inside is ensnared.

Goal

Rise from 3,000 m to the surface. Clear each wave of creatures by enclosing them in loops of light. Bigger loops with more creatures inside score multiplied points. Loop again quickly to build a chain.

Danger

Touching a creature costs one light. Lose them all and the dark wins. Spiny creatures and eels cut your thread, so loop around them, not through them.

Surge

Collect pearls to charge Surge. While it lasts, your thread can't be cut, it grows longer, you score double and you are untouchable.

Controls

  • Touch Drag anywhere like a trackpad. Tap Surge (bottom-right) with another finger.
  • Mouse Your light follows the cursor. Click or Space for Surge.
  • Keys WASD / arrows to swim · Space Surge · Esc/P pause
  • Gamepad Left stick · A Surge · Start pause
